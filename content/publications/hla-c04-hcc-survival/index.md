This brief research report examined whether HLA allotype was associated with
post-surgical survival among an ethnically diverse cohort of patients treated for
hepatocellular carcinoma in Hawaiʻi. In multivariable analysis, the presence of at
least one HLA-C*04 allele was associated with worse postoperative overall survival,
supporting further investigation of immunogenomic mechanisms in this population.
