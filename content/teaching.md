      title: 'Clinical AI and Data Science Education'
      subtitle: ''
      text: |-
        I am developing an educational program that connects foundational
        biostatistics with practical and responsible Clinical AI. The curriculum
        is designed to help health professionals understand how clinical data are
        generated, evaluate model performance and bias, distinguish prediction
        from causal inference, and collaborate effectively with quantitative and
        AI specialists.

        Current teaching activities include workshops on multiple testing,
        missing-data mechanisms, multiple imputation, sensitivity analysis, and
        reproducible implementation in R, together with applied machine-learning
        examples from healthcare research.
